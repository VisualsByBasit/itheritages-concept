## Home page — section order

1. Hero (3D network, headline, CTA, scroll cue)
2. Trust bar (certifications, partner marks)
3. Services overview (10 modules, links to individual service pages)
4. Industries overview (12 cards, links to individual industry pages)
5. Process / methodology
6. Stats (real numbers only)
7. Case studies teaser (or honest "coming soon" state)
8. AI Advisor prompt / callout
9. Contact CTA
10. Footer

## Individual service page — section order

1. Service hero (name, one-line positioning, relevant 3D/motion motif)
2. What it solves (business problem framing, not feature dump)
3. What's included (concrete capabilities — pull from current site's per-service pages so nothing gets lost in translation)
4. Related industries (cross-link)
5. CTA → contact / consultation

## Individual industry page — section order

1. Industry hero
2. Sector-specific challenges
3. Recommended solution set (maps back to the 10 services)
4. CTA → contact
